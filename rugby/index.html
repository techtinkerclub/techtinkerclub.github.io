Checks, assumptions and law references

Pass paths are deliberately checked against the direction of attack, not the hands or momentum of a real player. Offside and contact positions use simplified icon footprints; we cannot judge a player's actual body position, interference or the legality of a tackle. Offside guides appear only at an authored support/ruck formation with the ball on the ground. Entry checks compare positions before arrival. Spacing samples are optional and do not detect every collision.

The U10 age-specific checks use the 2026–27 RugbyReferee.net summary of RFU Regulation 15 Appendix 4 (not independently verified against the full official appendix). Other ages receive geometric and data checks, not a complete age-grade rules assessment. Animation seconds are not match-law timing. No obstruction or tactical judgement is attempted.
