About this equipment

The Heating Rate Controller provides two independent temperature-control channels for externally powered DC heaters. Learn what each channel does, how it connects, and how to operate it under the site's approved procedure.

Control each channel independently

Each channel has an Omron temperature controller, a maintained CONTROL selector, a momentary HEATER ENABLE button and a HEAT ACTIVE indicator. The temperature controller monitors a Type K thermocouple and commands the heater through the control circuit.

Channel 1Channel 2

Connect the external heater supply

The red-and-black connectors are arranged in two pairs per channel. Connect the external DC supply to the upper pair marked PSU IN. Connect the heater to the bottom pair marked HEATER OUT. Use the matching green Type K connector for temperature feedback.

Specified design envelope: up to 0–60 V DC and 15 A per heater channel. The final connector and protection ratings must be checked before operation.

Before first use

Before applying heater power, check the control wiring, protective devices, isolation arrangements and the approved test procedure. Electrical maintenance guidance.

    Three circuits to keep separate

    230 V AC supplies the controllers and Mean Well PSU. The 24 V circuit operates the enable relays and SSR inputs. External 0–60 V DC supplies the heaters through independent protection and switching.

    The ACTIVE indicators show demand, not measured heater current. Each channel's Weidmüller-controlled SSR return is different from permanent 0 V.

    Finder ENABLE control

    Finder 13.61 relays operate in RI step-relay mode. The momentary EAO ENABLE button toggles each channel. The white selector sets operating mode; the orange timer is for IT mode, not normal RI operation.

    Both channels have been reported functional. Electrical inspection and supply-restoration checks remain standard installation requirements.

    Standard operating procedure

    Use the approved site operating procedure for live heater tests. Verify wiring, protection and safe isolation before operation.

    Before operating a heater

    Check the external DC supply, protective devices, temperature input and the required safe-isolation procedure. Follow the site electrical inspection and operating requirements.

    01 · Before switching on

    1
    Inspect the test arrangement

    Check heater and cable condition, protective covers, cooling/clearance and the approved test configuration.

    2
    Check channel connections

    Match the green TC INPUT connector to the correct channel. The upper red/black pair is PSU IN; the lowest pair is HEATER OUT. Verify polarity and that power is isolated while connecting leads.

    3
    Check the power source and setpoint

    Confirm the external supply voltage/current limits and the Omron's sensor type, programme/setpoint and approved control parameters.

    02 · Begin a controlled run

    4
    Apply control power

    Use the approved mains/control energising sequence. Check the Omron display for a credible temperature reading and absence of fault indications.

    5
    Select the required channel

    Turn the relevant CONTROL selector ON and verify the heater remains disabled until the separate enable command is given.

    6
    Enable and monitor heating

    Following the approved procedure, use ENABLE and the Omron programme or setpoint to initiate heating. Monitor the displayed temperature, voltage and demand indication throughout.

    03 · End a run

    Remove heating demand and disable the channel by the approved shutdown sequence. Isolate the external heater supply, confirm the output is de-energised and allow the heater to cool before handling it. Use the separate site emergency-stop and fault-response instructions if abnormal behaviour occurs.

    Do not move, disconnect or reconnect the DC heater or thermocouple leads while their circuit is energised. Record the test configuration, parameters and any abnormal events.

    Power interruption

    The heater enable circuit must remain OFF after control power is restored. Confirm correct behaviour as part of commissioning; do not rely on controller RESET alone.

    Omron E5CC-T — setup and autotune

    Use this section when setting up a channel or checking its performance. Both channels are specified with the Omron E5CC-TQX3A5M-000. Parameter visibility and approved values still depend on the configured operating mode, protections and firmware.

    Controller setup

    1. Identify the fitted E5CC part number and confirm which channel is being configured.
    2. Verify that the current input in-t matches the actual physical sensor, its permitted range/resolution, the units and the global upper/lower SP limits. The Windows app can plan other documented sensors, but cannot identify the external probe by USB.
    3. Check that the displayed process value is plausible and that sensor errors are handled correctly.
    4. Confirm the control method, heating direction and output assignment for the installed SSR drive circuit.
    5. Check the control period, required limits and any configured alarms.
    6. Enter and read back the approved programme using d.prg and segment fields. Then, in Operation Level RESET, select the intended RUN programme using prg and verify pgsg.
    7. Record the final configuration and confirm it independently for the other channel.

    For full button-by-button instructions, open the linked E5CC-T programming guide. Verify the settings on each physical controller during commissioning.

    Autotune procedure

    1. Only start autotune after commissioning, with the real heater, thermocouple placement and representative thermal load in place.
    2. Assess the risk of temperature overshoot and confirm the independent safeguards before tuning.
    3. Choose an appropriate tuning target and begin the Omron's autotune function as specified in its user manual.
    4. Leave the arrangement stable while tuning operates. Do not change the test load or sensor positions.
    5. When tuning finishes, record the resulting control settings and evaluate a controlled heating response.
    6. If temperature oscillates or overshoots excessively, stop and investigate rather than repeatedly retuning.

    Autotune is not a substitute for verifying safety limits. It is unsuitable during an actual thermal-runaway event.

    Identify the actual controller

    USB 0503 returns only a 10-character prefix, for example E5CC-TQX3A. It does not identify 5M or the final -NNN option code. Check the printed nameplate and choose its exact full model in the Windows dropdown.

    The app uses the documented model option matrix to show only plausible event input, CT, RS-485, transfer-output and other option settings. An option still needs a successful actual register read and a qualified write procedure; selecting it is not proof of physical fitment or wiring.

    Connected workflow · one controller at a time

    1
    Connect and identify

    Read the USB model prefix and controller status; confirm the nameplate suffix for this connection. Start in read-only mode; enable writes only during an authorised isolated setup procedure.

    2
    Read and back up

    Capture all eight DA programme memories (150 registers each) and supported C0/C1/C3/C5 settings. Failed or unavailable hardware options are not interpreted as zero. Retain the backup and recorded controller identity.

    3
    Edit programme

    View the commanded SP curve, edit freely and compare all targets against the actual verified input range, decimal resolution and sl-h/sl-l limits. A conflict produces a warning and an offer to propose a compatible global sensor range, never an automatic rewrite.

    4
    Review controller settings

    Optional hardware parameters follow the confirmed full model and successful readback. Programmes, sensor choices, PID parameters, output assignments and safety settings are not all interchangeable write operations; unsupported or hazardous changes are kept read-only.

    5
    Apply and verify separately

    For a proposed C3 input change, preview the exact selected registers, isolate heater supplies, apply/read back in setting area 1, use Finish + Return to normal, visually verify normal PV/SP, then reconnect and perform a fresh Step 2 read/backup. Only then preview, write RAM, verify, save and finish the selected DA programme. Do not confuse CMW OFF with leaving setting area 1 or with heater disable.

    Sensor selection affects every programme: the Omron's existing DA words may acquire different physical meaning after changing input resolution even though the raw memory does not change. The app must not silently rescale any of the eight stored profiles. Physical sensor compatibility, output-off state at RESET, and power-return inhibition remain independent commissioning checks.

    Safety and maintenance

    Both channels are reported functional. The controls, heater connections and external supplies still require the normal checks for each installation and test.

    Before service or a new installation

    Isolate and prove dead on both the mains inlet and the separate 0–60 V DC heater supplies. Maintain cabinet and door protective-earth bonding, verify the specified component ratings, and ensure the heater-off state and power restoration are tested.
