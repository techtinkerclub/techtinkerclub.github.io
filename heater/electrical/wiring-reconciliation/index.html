WORKSHEET A · MAINS AND PROTECTIVE EARTH

One cabinet, three L take-offs and three N take-offs

The three Live distribution take-offs are PSU, Channel 1 selector input and Channel 2 selector input. The three Neutral distribution take-offs are PSU, E1 and E2. In addition, there are two switched-live wires from the selectors to their Omrons, an incoming L/N feed, and individual protective-earth bonds. The take-off counts are not the complete number of individual conductors.

230 V AC. X1 is a logical distribution name, not an allocated numbered terminal strip. Verify fused/switched inlet poles, selector contact markings, conductor sizing, branch protection, PE continuity and the actual PS1/Omron terminals. Never bridge PE and N or AC neutral and 24 V DC 0 V.

WORKSHEET D · SUPPLY DISTRIBUTION, JUNCTIONS & RELEASE CHECKS

Distribution counts, actual junction choices and checks

230 V mains distribution — cabinet total

3 X1 Live outgoing positions: PS1 L; S1 mains contact input; S2 mains contact input. 3 X1 Neutral outgoing positions: PS1 N; E1 N; E2 N. Also: J1 incoming L/N, selector outputs to E1/E2 Live, separate PE distribution and bonding.

Whether the installed strips have sufficient physical terminals depends on how the incoming and cross-connected feeds are landed. Count both input and output positions. Never treat N as PE.

24 V distribution — per channel

3 gated +24 V take-off positions and 3 common 0 V take-off positions reported as physically available, not yet inspected.

Illustrative grouping: +1 K1 A1 and 11; +2 SB1 COM and Crydom +3; +3 ACTIVE+; 0V1 K1 A2; 0V2 K3 A2 and ENABLE LED−; 0V3 Weidmüller 14. Alternative approved jumpers are permitted. EN1/EN2 are distinct relay-enabled junctions.

Do not confuse the three positive circuits

+24 shared: always available from PS1 while control supply is on. +24 gated: available after the channel's CONTROL selector. EN1/EN2: only available after Finder 13.61 output 14 closes; supplies K3/K4 A1 and EAO ENABLE LED+.

The ACTIVE lamp is the SSR demand indicator, not actual heater-current proof.

Weidmüller verified topology

Input A1 (+12 V)/A2 (−12 V) is driven by the Omron SSR command. Output transistor terminals 13 (+) and 14 (−) complete the series path +24 gated → Crydom +3 / ACTIVE+ → Crydom −4 / ACTIVE− → W13 → W14 → 0V. Its manufacturer gives 5–48 V DC load, 100 mA maximum; Crydom input up to 15 mA and proposed ACTIVE lamp up to 20 mA (35 mA combined worst-case), subject to actual installed lamp/type and protection.

Connector / ferrule rules

Do not assume two conductors are allowed in one clamp. Check the exact terminal-block maker's permitted conductor count, twin-ferrule dimensions, clamp capacity, strip length and torque. The Weidmüller output is PUSH IN; manufacturer instructions depict two side-by-side inserted wires as incorrect. Use approved distribution junctions where necessary. No X1–X4 numbered terminal assignment has been confirmed.

Mandatory evidence before release

  • Photograph stamped terminals on both Finder 13.61 devices and the EAO contact blocks
  • Verify actual X1–X4 terminal-strip and junction allocation; apply wire markers
  • Dead continuity/insulation and PE bonding test by competent person
  • Verify Weidmüller polarity, SSR demand and LEDs with a safe, low-energy test
  • Prove control-power loss/return cannot re-enable heater, including held pushbutton
  • Verify emergency isolation, protection coordination, 15 A duty and heat dissipation
  • Record as-built, independent review, and revision approval
