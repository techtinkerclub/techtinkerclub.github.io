D13 drawing archive

Eleven-sheet drawing set made by editing the original D12 PowerPoint drawings, not replacing them with newly styled block diagrams. Original shapes, line routing, colours, sheet sequence, manufacturer links and overall layout are retained. Corrected terminals and connection annotations are incorporated in their original schematic positions.

Inspection draft — not released as-built. Physical terminal markings, distribution block capacities and any unfinished channel wiring must be independently verified before commissioning. D13 incorporates confirmed manufacturer terminal definitions and builder-reported observations, but a report of a completed connection is not an inspection result.

What changed from D12?

Original sheetsD13 amendments
01 · MainsThree Live outgoing destinations and three Neutral outgoing destinations for the whole cabinet, plus separate incoming feed, controller switched-Live leads and protective earth.
02 · Shared 24 VThree reported +24 V and three 0 V available distribution take-off positions per channel. Exact block allocation and approved junction methods remain to check.
03–04 · Finder enableReplace incorrect Finder 13.61 terminal markings L/+ and N/− with actual A1 and A2; distinguish EAO pushbutton COM from its illuminated LED+; propose RI mode and require restart tests.
05–06 · SSR driveLabel Crydom control +3 as sharing the gated +24 V / EAO COM, ACTIVE+ on that same rail; Crydom −4 and ACTIVE− join Weidmüller 13, while Weidmüller 14 returns to 0 V. Channel 2 remains individually unverified.
07–08 · Heater loadKeep original high-current power path drawings and emphasize Finder 62.31 34(+)→14(−) and Crydom +1→−2 are the switched POSITIVE load path, not DC 0 V.
09–11 · SchedulesRevise mains, controls, topology notes, unresolved configuration settings and inspection priorities.

Independent inspection

Use the wire-by-wire reconciliation register alongside these drawings. The checker must compare actual terminal stamps and conductor terminations with the diagram; record deviations, uninspected points, tests and signatures. Do not infer approval from the document revision number.
