DIN-rail mounting and conductor insertion

Mounting selection

The selected PTFIX …-NS35 variants are supplied ready for 35 mm top-hat DIN rail, NS 35/7.5 or NS 35/15. No separate PTFIX-to-DIN adapter is required for the selected ready-to-mount part numbers.

The WAGO 2002-1407 ground terminal is also specified for 35 × 7.5 or 35 × 15 DIN rail. Verify the rail, PE contact, mounting stability and bonding of the complete assembly.

Use the lengthwise -NS35A family only if the bill of materials and physical layout are revised to match; mounting orientations are not interchangeable part numbers.

Wiring practice

PTFIX 2.5 family: push-in termination, nominal cross-section 2.5 mm², rigid/flexible conductor range per selected manufacturer data. Phoenix specifies 8–10 mm stripping length; ferruled flexible wires must match the manufacturer's permitted ferrule and conductor range.

Use one conductor per marked port. Keep the mains, protective earth and low-voltage circuits separated and properly enclosed. Leave safe room around each connector for insertion, withdrawal, wire bend radius and inspection.

No conductive bridging between separate distribution groups. Do not use PTFIX current ratings to justify the external 15 A heater circuit or reduce protective-device requirements.

Manufacturer references: Phoenix Contact PTFIX catalogue · PTFIX 12-way technical data · WAGO 2002-1407 technical data.

DC input voltmeters · PV1 and PV2

Each Murata DMR20-10-DCM-R-C meter measures its channel's external DC supply input at TB1. The meter has an internal self-resetting fuse. The manufacturer also requires external 0.25 A / 300 V time-delay protection for the input wiring.

Fitted configuration: no separate external meter fuses have been installed. Do not mark these branches as externally protected in wiring records. The two meter connections are not included in the 48-port PTFIX terminal schedule or the existing 70-route connection table.

Inspection and commissioning checks

  • Verify block part numbers, mounting, secure retention, markings, insulation, conductor insertion and all 48 terminations against the schedule.
  • Verify mains L/N segregation, protective-earth continuity, cabinet/door bonds, conductor sizing and fault protection.
  • Confirm X2-0V is permanent control return; X3-DRV1/X4-DRV2 are demand-switched outputs, not permanent DC 0 V.
  • Verify Finder 13.61 K1/K2 terminal 14 is the supply to the matching ACTIVE LED positive; confirm lamp truth table for both channels.
  • Verify external DC source polarity and switching direction, DC protection, Finder 62.31/socket suitability at maximum duty and Crydom thermal derating.
  • Record that the fitted Murata meters lack external input-wire fuses, and have a competent person assess and resolve the protection discrepancy against the manufacturer instructions before approval.
  • Verify Omron RESET removes heat demand, safe power-restoration behaviour, and all required emergency isolation and temperature protection.
  • Before operation, complete the equipment risk assessment, operating procedure release checks and any specimen-specific controls.

These are acceptance and maintenance checks. The reference design is not evidence of independent electrical sign-off.
