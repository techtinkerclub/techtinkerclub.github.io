Service, replacement and modification

Use this workflow when replacing a failed unit or introducing a design change. Revalidate the original circuit and assess any newly introduced risks.

1. Identify and isolate

Record channel, fault symptoms and electrical schematic revision. Isolate and verify de-energisation of both the AC control supply and the separate external DC supply, including stored energy; identify all affected terminals.

2. Match the replacement

Use the exact part number and voltage/type, not just physical appearance. Check Omron output variant, Finder contact/coil/socket, SSR polarity, DC breaker rating and the LED current limit.

3. Document original connections

Before removal, photograph terminal locations, conductor IDs, selector contact blocks and protective earth points. Capture the Omron parameters and program recipes.

4. Install and inspect

Confirm datasheet torque, terminal arrangement, conductor size, required clearances, mechanical support and earth continuity. Update any damaged labels and cable IDs.

5. Test without a live heater

Use approved dead tests and suitable low-energy functional checks. Verify independent channel control, SSR command and LED operation, 24 V reset and loss/return-of-power response.

6. Release a revised drawing

Record replacement date, fitted suffix/serial, changed nets and test results. Issue an updated circuit revision and operating procedure; re-approve before a powered test.

Outstanding hardware details.Physical Schurter terminals, X1–X4 distribution blocks and jumpers, actual Schneider second-contact blocks, Finder 62.31 socket suffix and suppressor, protection coordination, SSR thermal design and emergency disconnection remain to be verified.
