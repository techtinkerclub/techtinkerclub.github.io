Engineering cross-check and release blockers

The uploaded D12 is the controlling design-review snapshot, not a signed-off installation drawing. The issues below are called out for reconciliation with the actual parts and hardware, not silently changed in the source.

ItemReview requirement / why it matters
Omron E2 variantD12 sheets 01 and 06 list E5CC-TQX3A5M-003 for Channel 2, while newer operator documentation describes two -000 controllers. Omron confirms -003 is a distinct genuine variant with additional RS-485/heater-alarm capability. Inspect both physical nameplates and correct the documents once fitted models are confirmed.
Finder 13.61 start/resetValidate OFF-after-control-power-restoration, short interruptions, and a held ENABLE button. The fact that p-on = rst was observed on an isolated USB-powered E5CC-T does not validate the heater-enable relay circuit.
Socket and DC continuityConfirm installed Finder 92.03 suffix and the actual A1/A2/34/14 mapping, continuous DC current/ambient derating and mechanical terminal rating. The intended 15 A channel duty is close to a nominal 16 A switching/socket family.
Weidmüller and lampSelected HL1/HL2: RS PRO 130-1086, yellow ACTIVE LED, 24 V DC, 20 mA maximum, 14 mm mounting hole, IP67, chrome bezel, lead wires. Calculated maximum combined draw per channel: 20 mA LED + 15 mA SSR = 35 mA, below Weidmüller 8950770000's 100 mA DC1 rating. The part is identified, not confirmed installed. Verify fitted item, polarity, exact wiring, actual switching behavior and operating margin. The manual illustration depicts the manufacturer's yellow lens as amber.
Connector namesD12 sheets 07–08 use JP1-IN/OUT and JP2-IN/OUT as channel-pair labels, while the front-panel manual uses separate JP1/JP2 for Channel 1 and JP3/JP4 for Channel 2. Agree a single physical designation schedule before ferrule assignment; do not modify connectivity based solely on identifiers.
Protection and coolingVerify Schurter output tabs/fuses, X1–X4 stock parts/jumpers and PE, DC breaker coordination, external supply prospective fault current, 15 A conductor and connector capacity, Crydom SSR heat dissipation, emergency disconnection and independently tested sensor-fault behaviour.

Service, replacement and modification

Use this workflow when replacing a failed unit or introducing a design change. Revalidate the original circuit and assess any newly introduced risks.

1. Identify and isolate

Record channel, fault symptoms and electrical schematic revision. Isolate and verify de-energisation of both the AC control supply and the separate external DC supply, including stored energy; identify all affected terminals.

2. Match the replacement

Use the exact part number and voltage/type, not just physical appearance. Check Omron output variant, Finder contact/coil/socket, SSR polarity, DC breaker rating and the LED current limit.

3. Document original connections

Before removal, photograph terminal locations, conductor IDs, selector contact blocks and protective earth points. Capture the Omron parameters and program recipes.

4. Install and inspect

Confirm datasheet torque, terminal arrangement, conductor size, required clearances, mechanical support and earth continuity. Update any damaged labels and cable IDs.

5. Test without a live heater

Use approved dead tests and suitable low-energy functional checks. Verify independent channel control, SSR command and LED operation, 24 V reset and loss/return-of-power response.

6. Release a revised drawing

Record replacement date, fitted suffix/serial, changed nets and test results. Issue an updated circuit revision and operating procedure; re-approve before a powered test.

Omron reset-output check. On both E5CC-T controllers, confirm p-on = rst and rtsm = stop, and verify mv-r = 0.0% as applicable. The reset output can be configured independently of the programme reset state. None of these settings replaces a physical restart-inhibit interlock.
Outstanding hardware details.Physical Schurter terminals, X1–X4 distribution blocks and jumpers, actual Schneider second-contact blocks, Finder 62.31 socket suffix and suppressor, protection coordination, SSR thermal design and emergency disconnection remain to be verified.
