How the electrical system is arranged

One AC inlet supplies the shared 24 V control power supply and the two Omron controllers. Each channel has its own ENABLE button, Finder switching relays, Crydom SSR, activity indicator, thermocouple and external 0–60 V DC heater circuit.

1 · Shared control power

230 V AC inlet → separate L/N branches → Mean Well 24 V PSU and the two independently selected Omron AC feeds.

2 · Channel enable and demand

Selector → EAO momentary enable → Finder 13.61 → Finder 62.31. Omron demand drives the Weidmüller interface, Crydom SSR and ACTIVE lamp.

3 · External heater power

DC+ passes through protection, the correctly oriented Finder power contact, the Crydom SSR and the heater. The external DC− returns directly.

Follow an individual channel

The two circuits are identical in function and isolated in their enable/SSR drive connections. Select a channel to display its device references.

Three connections must not be confused: X2-0V is permanent control return; X3-DRV1/X4-DRV2 are demand-switched returns via Weidmüller 13→14; external heater DC− is a separate power return. Finder 62.31 contact 14 (−) and Crydom load −2 are both on the switched positive DC path.

Maintenance essentials

  • Isolate and prove dead on both the AC inlet and the separate external DC heater supply before opening or changing the cabinet.
  • Match exact component model, voltage, terminal position, conductor rating and polarity before replacement.
  • Bond the door, cabinet body and PSU protective earth; verify continuity of the protective-earth path.
  • Preserve the independently switched channel enables and the directional 62.31 contact polarity: supply DC+ → 34 (+), outgoing DC+ → 14 (−).
  • After a wiring change, independently check protection, switching, emergency isolation, insulation, PE continuity, heater-off state and restart behaviour.

The system has been reported functioning on both channels. This does not replace electrical inspection or authorise bypassing any protective device.
