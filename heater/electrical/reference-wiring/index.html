Terminal-by-terminal assignments

ACTIVE indicator wiring update · 26 October 2026. The operator reports moving the amber ACTIVE LED positive from selector-switched +24 V to the corresponding Finder 13.61 terminal 14 (EN output); its Weidmüller-controlled negative return stays unchanged. This matches R1. Confirm which channel(s) were modified and check the indicator behavior on each channel; operation and correct wiring are not independently verified.

Rebuild standard: K1:14 → X3-EN1 → HL1:+ (Channel 1), K2:14 → X4-EN2 → HL2:+ (Channel 2); each HL negative returns via its own Weidmüller demand output. The operator reports connecting directly to terminal 14; this does not confirm that the specified distribution blocks are physically fitted. Use correctly rated terminal distribution and connection methods in a rebuild.

Open a block to see each assigned position. CH1 = X3, CH2 = X4. Unallocated positions remain spare.

Complete circuit paths

Mains and 24 V

J1 inlet L/N/PE → X1-L/X1-N/X1-PE → Mean Well PS1 (24 V) and two separately selected Omron controller AC feeds. PS1 +Vo → X2-24 → S1-B and S2-B. PS1 −Vo → X2-0V.

Enable, each channel

S1-B/S2-B gated +24 V → X3-24G/X4-24G → Finder 13.61 L/+ and terminal 11, plus EAO C. Pressing EAO NO sends a pulse to Finder terminal 3. Finder output 14 → EN1/EN2 → Finder 62.31 coil A1, EAO LED+, Crydom A1/+3 and amber HL+.

Temperature demand

Type K (+) → Omron 6; Type K (−) → Omron 5. Omron output 1(+), 2(−) → Weidmüller input A1(+), A2(−). Crydom A2/−4 and HL− → DRV1/DRV2 → Weidmüller 13(+); Weidmüller 14(−) → common X2-0V.

External heater power

External DC+ → Lovato breaker 1→2 → Finder 62.31 contact 34 (+)→14 (−) → Crydom load +1→−2 → HEATER+. HEATER− goes straight to matching external DC−. The Finder contact 14 (−) and Crydom load −2 are on the switched POSITIVE path.

Functional note: CH1 and CH2 operate independently; they share the Mean Well 24 V supply. The amber ACTIVE lamp requires ENABLE ON and Omron SSR demand; it does not measure actual heater current. The Finder 13.61 is used as a momentary-enable step relay in RI mode.

Components and rebuild notes

Control

  • E1/E2: Omron E5CC-T, one per channel
  • PS1: Mean Well XDR-120E-24, shared 24 V supply
  • S1/S2: Schneider Harmony XB5AD23, two contact sets per channel
  • SB1/SB2: EAO momentary illuminated enable buttons
  • K1/K2: Finder 13.61 step/impulse relays
  • SK3/SK4: Finder 62.31.9.024.4800 with compatible socket

Demand and heater power

  • W1/W2: Weidmüller 8950770000 interface
  • SSR1/SSR2: Crydom DR2220D30U
  • HL1/HL2: 24 V amber ACTIVE indicator
  • QF1/QF2: Lovato P1MB1PC16 DC protection reference
  • External heater supply: 0–60 V DC, 15 A maximum design limit/channel
  • PV1/PV2: Murata DMR20-10-DCM-R-C, RS 810-3321, 6–75 VDC
  • JP1–JP4: Electro PJP 3266-I-R / 3266-I-N, RS 268-9422 / 268-9421 (4 red and 4 black)
  • JTC1/JTC2: RS PRO 455-9742, IEC Type K panel sockets
  • Protective earth: cabinet and door separately bonded via PE block

Meter protection: the Murata documentation requires external 0.25 A / 300 V time-delay fusing of the meter input supply wiring. Those individual meter take-offs were not included in the first R1 distribution count or 70-row wiring worksheet; treat those figures as the original reference allocation, not a complete updated conductor tally. Fuseholder choice, wiring positions and DC fault-interruption ratings require a new issue.

For installation or replacement, check the manufacturer's terminal diagrams, actual device variants, PE continuity, conductor ratings, AC/DC protection, DC switching polarity, SSR thermal capacity, and power-restoration behaviour. Functional operation does not replace an independent electrical inspection. Isolate both mains and external DC supplies before maintenance.

Reference Wiring R1.1 is the intended standard configuration, not a survey of every installed wire. Earlier D12 and D13 drawings are retained as historical documentation in the drawing archive.
