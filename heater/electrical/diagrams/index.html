Four electrical circuits

Each circuit is shown separately so the control supply, heating command and external DC power cannot be mistaken for the same electrical path. Device references correspond to the numbered connection diagrams below.

The external heater DC supply is independent of the 24 V controller supply. Finder 62.31 terminal 14 (−) and Crydom load terminal −2 are on the switched positive heater path, not on the DC negative return.
