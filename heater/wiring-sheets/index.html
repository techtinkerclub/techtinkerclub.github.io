ACTIVE indicator wiring update · 26 October 2026. The operator reports moving the amber ACTIVE LED positive from selector-switched +24 V to the corresponding Finder 13.61 terminal 14 (EN output); its Weidmüller-controlled negative return stays unchanged. This matches R1. Confirm which channel(s) were modified and check the indicator behavior on each channel; operation and correct wiring are not independently verified. Reference paths: K1:14 → X3-EN1:5 → HL1:+ and K2:14 → X4-EN2:5 → HL2:+. HL1:− and HL2:− continue to Weidmüller demand-switched returns. These R1 connections were already specified; the electrical schematic does not change.

Reading a row: FROM identifies the source point, VIA the allocated terminal or intermediate cable path, and TO the destination or electrical net. Square brackets, such as [X3-EN1 common net], mean the internal electrically common potential of that block, not an extra wire. Where the block itself appears in VIA, the row represents one physical terminated conductor.
