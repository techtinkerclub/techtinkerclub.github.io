Printable wiring worksheets

Eight A4 sheets · D12 circuit references · 39 design nets · designed for use beside the cabinet.

← Electrical reference
Design review, not released as-built wiring. Match each proposed connection to the D12 schematic and the stamped terminal on the actual fitted component before termination. X1–X4 are logical net names, not verified physical block positions. Multi-destination rows can require several separate wires. Both AC and external heater DC supplies must be isolated during installation and inspection. Finder 13.61 correction (22 Oct 2026): the original D12 PDF's L/+ and N/− are rail labels, not physical K1/K2 terminal markings. On type 13.61.0.024.0000, confirm A1 (+24 V), A2 (0 V), 3, ON, OFF and contact numbers 11/12/14 against the Finder diagram. Verify the exact fitted variant before connecting.
