Scope, users and status

Applies to preparation, routine supervised heating, shutdown, cleaning, and permitted maintenance of the laboratory heating-rate controller. Intended users: trained test operators, responsible electrical engineer, site safety team and authorised maintenance technicians.

Assessment status: draft for site review — not approved for live operation. Both channels have been reported functionally operating. This is not the same as evidence of independent electrical protection checks, fail-safe output behaviour, overheating protection or suitability for every test specimen. Risk controls below must be implemented and signed off locally. This document is not a certification.

Experiment-specific hazards: heating a battery, reactive material, pressurised vessel or combustible assembly can cause a hazard even after power is removed. A separate specimen/process assessment must define containment, ventilation/extraction, remote operation, fire and emergency procedures before the test. This equipment assessment does not authorise thermal-runaway or destructive tests by itself.

Risk scoring to be completed by site assessor

Assign likelihood and severity in accordance with your site's approved method, considering the actual installation, environment, exposure and implemented controls. Residual risk is deliberately not pre-scored as 'low' before the required protection tests and specimen assessment are complete.

Two-channel capability approval

Assessment follows the HSE principle: identify hazards, assess who may be harmed, implement controls, record findings and review controls when equipment or tests change. UK HSE risk assessment guidance ↗

Evidence required before routine live tests

The following are practical release checks, not a statement that they have been passed.

  • □ Electrical inspection: mains/PE bonding, insulation, approved circuit protection, cable ratings and accessible independent DC isolation.
  • □ Verify Omron r-r RESET really removes demand on both channels (rtsm=stop, mv-r=0%, sensor fault response).
  • □ Power-restoration test: both channel enable outputs OFF; Omron starts in RESET; no automatic heating.
  • □ Verify independent over-temperature, ventilation/containment, and test-specific measures for the intended specimen.
  • □ Confirm 62.31 and socket suitability at actual DC current/duty, Crydom heat dissipation and fusing on Murata meter inputs.
  • □ Verify both channels: CONTROL selector, EAO toggle, EN ring, amber ACTIVE gated by EN + demand, output switching and safe shutdown.

Do not rely on EAO heater enable as a safety-rated emergency stop. Site emergency isolation must act on the relevant independent energy source, without requiring the operator to approach a hot or faulted test.

Record for each experiment

  • □ Test identifier, operator(s), specimen, fixture and specimen-specific hazard assessment.
  • □ Channel(s), selected programme number, ramp/peak/soak, control limits, planned endpoint and sample temperature limit.
  • □ External PSU model, maximum voltage and current-limit setting, heater/cable/connector rating.
  • □ Visual inspection, temperature sensor PV validation, safe isolation arrangement, independent temperature/cooling.
  • □ Start/stop times; Omron segment and event record; abnormal results; sign-off and post-test cool down.

Review this assessment following alterations to wiring/components, new specimen categories, any unexpected heating or faults, an incident or near miss, or changes to the work area/protective equipment.

Review and authorisation

Risk scoring, adequacy and effectiveness of controls, responsible persons, due dates and sign-off must be completed by competent local reviewers. Record reference to the approved site-specific specimen assessment.
